# Venom-Bullet-SPT-4.1.3
A rare experimental 5.56x45 cartridge loaded with a highly unstable venom compound designed to spread rapidly through the body after penetrating armor or exposed tissue. Once introduced into the bloodstream, the toxin begins a progressive reaction that can cause nausea, tremors, severe weakness, loss of stamina, impaired movement, tunnel vision, and eventual systemic failure. The effects worsen over time and can ultimately prove fatal if left untreated.

The compound is not immediately lethal, giving the victim a short window to react before the symptoms become overwhelming. Antibiotics can neutralize the infection if taken quickly enough, making immediate treatment essential. Head impacts and rounds completely stopped by armor do not transmit the toxin.

Extremely scarce and reportedly manufactured only in small clandestine batches, Venom ammunition is considered both highly valuable and dangerously unpredictable.
